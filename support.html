Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

Go to the login screen and tap "Forgot Password". Enter your email and we'll send you a reset link.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your device's app store. On iOS, go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. On Android, go to Google Play Store > Payments & Subscriptions.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile > Settings > scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account". This action is permanent and will erase all your data.

The AI coach isn't responding

Make sure you have an active internet connection. If the issue persists, try closing and reopening the app. If you're on the free plan, you may have reached your daily usage limit.

My workout data seems incorrect

You can edit any workout session by tapping on it in your history. If data appears missing after an update, please contact support and we'll help recover it.

How do I change units (kg/lbs)?

Go to Profile > Settings and toggle between metric (kg) and imperial (lbs) units.
